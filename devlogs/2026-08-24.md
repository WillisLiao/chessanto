## Session goal

Execute `handoffs/NEXT-SESSION-THREAT-DETECTOR.md`: implement the ignored-threat detector (P4.2, scoped to one detector).
The goal is to detect when the mover's move ignored a concrete threat that the opponent already had (either winning material or delivering checkmate), where the mover failed to address it and the opponent carried it out on the subsequent move.

## Scoping

Per `handoffs/NEXT-SESSION-THREAT-DETECTOR.md`, scope was strictly bounded to one detector: `ThemeDetector.ignoredThreat`.
Forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, back-rank weaknesses, trapped pieces, move-quality flags, and other P4 items were explicitly out of scope.

## Design decisions

Following the five required design decisions in the bootstrap document:

1. **Fact shape and prose composition:**
   Added `IgnoredThreatFact` in `Facts.swift` (`Sendable, Equatable, Codable`) carrying `ply`, `threatenedSAN`, optional `capturedPieceKind` and `capturedSquare`, `netMaterialGainForOpponent`, and `isCheckmate`.
   Attached as `ignoredThreat: IgnoredThreatFact?` on `KeyMoment`.
   Rendered in `ReportText` using direct, qualitative prose:
   - Checkmate threat: `"This ignored the threat of checkmate: \(threatenedSAN)."`
   - Full piece won: `"This ignored the threat to the \(kind) on \(square): \(threatenedSAN) winning the \(kind)."`
   - Material won: `"This ignored the threat to the \(kind) on \(square): \(threatenedSAN) winning material."`
   - When both `punishment` (with `capturesJustMovedPiece == false`) and `ignoredThreat` describe the same target square, `ReportText` renders `ignoredThreat` and suppresses the redundant `punishment` sentence to prevent duplicating the exact same square and capture in one key-moment summary.

2. **`FactAuditor` integration:**
   Added `FactAuditor.verify(_ fact: IgnoredThreatFact, input: ReportInput) -> Bool` and wired it into `FactAuditor.audit(_ moment: KeyMoment, input: ReportInput) -> KeyMoment?`.
   The auditor independently re-derives the fact from board replay and checks `threatenedSAN`, `capturedPieceKind`, `capturedSquare`, `netMaterialGainForOpponent`, and `isCheckmate`.

3. **Classification vs KeyMoment attachment:**
   `ignoredThreat` attaches as an explanatory `Fact` on `KeyMoment` for moments already selected (the same model as `punishment`, `betterMove`, `missedMate`, and `allowedMate`), rather than inventing a new `MoveClassification`.

4. **Register interactions:**
   `IgnoredThreatFact` uses no percentages and no eval numerals, rendering cleanly for beginners, intermediate, and advanced users alike.

5. **`KeyMomentSelector.prefersNameableConsequences`:**
   Updated `hasConsequence` in `KeyMomentSelector.swift` to include `ThemeDetector.ignoredThreat(input: input, ply: ply) != nil`, ensuring beginner-register reports prioritize moments with an ignored concrete threat when capping at 4 moments.

## Operational definition and board replay logic

`ThemeDetector.ignoredThreat` stays strictly within stored replay data:
- Replays the opponent's actual played move at ply `p + 1` from the post-move position (`input.plies[p].fen`).
- Confirms the move was either an immediate checkmate or a capture.
- Switches the active color in the pre-move position (`input.plies[p - 1].fen`) to the opponent's color and replays the same opponent move.
- Confirms the move was already legal and achievable before the mover moved:
  - If checkmate, it was already an immediate checkmate in pre-move.
  - If a capture, it captured the same piece kind on the same square.
- For captures, calculates pre-move and post-move net material gain using `ChessGame.hasLegalMove` for recapture checks and relative piece values:
  - Undefended piece: full piece value.
  - Defended piece attacked by a lesser piece: value difference (e.g. Bishop taking Queen on a defended square nets +6).
  - Defended piece in an equal exchange (e.g. Bishop taking defended Bishop): net gain is 0 (equal trade), so the detector does not fire.
  - If the mover defended the target square, moved the target piece away, captured the attacker, or blocked the attack line, net gain becomes 0 or replay fails, so the detector does not fire.
