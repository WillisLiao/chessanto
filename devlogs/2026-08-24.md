## Session goal

Execute the Priority 5 batch of 8 independent small UI detail fixes from `handoffs/NEXT-SESSION-UI-POLISH.md` on the dedicated `ui-polish-p5` branch.
All changes are constrained to the App layer, strictly preserving the warm-neutral forced-light visual identity and staying out of `Packages/AnalysisKit` and `Packages/CoachKit`.

## Item-by-item resolution

### 1. Classification chip wrapping
- **Re-verification finding**: `ClassificationChip` lacked `.lineLimit(1)` and `.fixedSize(horizontal: true, vertical: false)`, allowing multi-word or long labels like "Inaccuracy" to wrap mid-word in narrow columns.
- **Change**: Added `.lineLimit(1)` and `.fixedSize(horizontal: true, vertical: false)` directly to `ClassificationChip` in `App/Sources/Chessanto/Analysis/MoveClassificationStyle.swift`.

### 2. Missing classification marks legend
- **Re-verification finding**: The 10 classification marks (star, thumbs up, checkmark, `?!`, `?`, `??`, `!!`, missed win `xmark`, book, forced) had no explanation in the report view.
- **Change**: Added a collapsible `DisclosureGroup` legend ("Classification marks legend") inside the Game Audit card in `App/Sources/Chessanto/GameReportView.swift`.
- All 10 cases are displayed using their `ClassificationChip` and copy sourced directly from `ChessGlossary.gloss(for:)`.

### 3. Accuracy formatting and user identification
- **Re-verification finding**: `accuracySummary` in `GameReplayView` displayed raw numbers without percent signs ("White 93.8 · Black 90.8") and gave no visual indication of which player was the user.
- **Change**: Created `AccuracySummaryFormatter` in `BoardIdentityStrip.swift` and exposed `BoardIdentityStrip.isUser` for case-insensitive username matching.
- Updated `accuracySummary` in `GameReplayView.swift` and `GameReportView.swift` to render `White (You) 93.8%` with accented styling for the user's side, backed by new unit tests in `BoardIdentityStripTests.swift`.

### 4. Sidebar result text
- **Re-verification finding**: When no chess.com username was set, `GameRow` fell back to rendering raw score strings like "1-0" or "0-1" in `outcomeIndicator`, which learners read as sports scores rather than game outcomes.
- **Change**: Added `GameRowMetadata.plainResult(_:)` to translate results into "White won", "Black won", and "Draw".
- Updated `outcomeIndicator` in `ContentView.swift` to render plain language with corresponding accessibility labels when no username perspective is available, backed by new unit tests in `GameRowMetadataTests.swift`.

### 5. LinesPanelView principal variation truncation and adopt action
- **Re-verification finding**: Clicking any engine line immediately adopted it into the variation tree without confirmation, while the line text was truncated with no way to inspect the full sequence.
- **Change**: Separated inspection from mutation in `App/Sources/Chessanto/Analysis/LinesPanelView.swift`.
- Added `.help(fullSANLine)` for full line visibility on hover and introduced a dedicated, non-intrusive branch button (`Image(systemName: "arrow.triangle.branch")`) with tooltip and accessibility label for intentional line adoption.

### 6. Eval graph key-moment dots hit target
- **Re-verification finding**: Key moment dots in `EvalGraphView` were bare 6pt circles with no independent tap/click hit target.
- **Change**: Wrapped each key moment circle in `App/Sources/Chessanto/Analysis/EvalGraphView.swift` in a button with a 24x24 point hit target (`.contentShape(Circle())`), triggering `onScrub(moment.ply)` on click with tooltip and VoiceOver label.

### 7. Eval bar scale mismatch
- **Re-verification finding**: The eval bar fill uses win probability (logistic sigmoid curve) while its numeric label shows pawn evaluation / mate distance in centipawns, with no explanation of the scale difference.
- **Design decision**: Kept the win probability bar fill (the established standard for chess UI responsiveness) and the exact pawn label, and added `.help(...)` and accessibility descriptions explaining that the bar fill indicates win probability while the label indicates pawn/mate evaluation.

### 8. Unexplained disabled button in PlayerBriefView
- **Re-verification finding**: When no practice cards existed, the "Practice positions" button in `PlayerBriefView.swift` was disabled without explanation.
- **Change**: Added a `.help` tooltip ("Analyze a game first to generate practice cards") and descriptive accessibility label when disabled.
