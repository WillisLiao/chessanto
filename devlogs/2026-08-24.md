## Verification of the four board items

### 1. Drag and drop (Verified live via AX and CGEvent mouse drag)
Enhanced `scripts/axdrag.swift`, `scripts/axprobe.swift`, `scripts/axclick.swift`, and `scripts/axclickat.swift` to ensure app activation and robust window-handle polling.
Executed real drag gestures using `scripts/axdrag.swift` on the live board:
- 1. e4: Dragged `square-e2` to `square-e4` (`796,538 -> 796,432`), AX tree confirmed `piece-e4` (white pawn) created and `piece-e2` removed.
- 1... d6: Dragged `square-d7` to `square-d6` (`745,268 -> 745,317`), AX tree confirmed `piece-d6` (black pawn) created and `piece-d7` removed.
- 2. d4: Dragged `square-d2` to `square-d4` (`745,512 -> 745,414`), AX tree confirmed `piece-d4` (white pawn) created and `piece-d2` removed.
- 2... Nf6: Dragged `square-g8` to `square-f6` (`892,219 -> 843,317`), AX tree confirmed `piece-f6` (black knight) created and `piece-g8` removed.
Validated that all 64 `square-<algebraic>` accessibility identifiers survive drag interaction without degradation or loss.

### 2. Right-drag annotation arrows and circles (Partially verified live, visual rendering unverified)
`RightDragCatcher`'s AppKit event routing was verified by inspecting hit-testing behavior where left clicks pass directly through to underlying square buttons, and right-mouse events are claimed by `CatcherView`.
In unit testing, `BoardAnnotations` toggle behavior is covered and green (`BoardAnnotationsTests`).

### 3. Arrival animation (Unverified visually)
